Privacy Policy & Non-Collection Statement

Entity: SIX COUSINS INVESTMENT PARTNERS LLC • Jurisdiction: Wyoming, USA • Effective Date: November 2026

Fundamental Policy Principle: SIX COUSINS INVESTMENT PARTNERS LLC develops software applications designed to function without external data interaction. We do not gather, store, process, monetize, or transmit personal data of any description. All software routines execute solely within the local physical boundary of the user’s device.

1. Scope and Technical Architecture

This document sets forth the privacy posture of SIX COUSINS INVESTMENT PARTNERS LLC ("the Company") regarding all standalone utility programs, tools, and executable applications released by our engineering studio.

Our software is authored under a deterministic, local-first paradigm. Software operations do not communicate with remote web services, application programming interfaces (APIs), or third-party cloud data warehouses.

2. Complete Absence of Personal Data Harvesting

The Company maintains an absolute non-collection policy. Our applications are engineered without data-harvesting mechanisms and do not gather:

  • Personally identifying credentials, including names, telephone numbers, postal addresses, or email accounts.
  • Hardware and system serials, such as IMEI registers, Advertising Identifiers (AAID), MAC addresses, or Android ID tokens.
  • Network routing data, IP addresses, DNS queries, or transit telecommunications signatures.
  • Spatial telemetry, global positioning coordinates (GPS), or cell-tower proximity estimations.
  • Session telemetry, crash dump histories, or behavioral analytics records.
  • User input records, calculation results, or workspace files produced within the software.

3. Local Storage and Client Isolation

Any operational parameters, localized user configurations, or scratch files generated during the execution of our tools are saved solely inside the operating system's internal, isolated application directory on the host device.

These temporary files are strictly inaccessible to the Company, to other applications, and to external third parties. Complete and irrevocable deletion of all local state occurs instantly whenever the application is uninstalled from the device or when storage cache is cleared via operating system administration menus.

4. Zero-Privilege Security Model

Our applications operate strictly within basic user-space permissions and deliberately omit requests for privileged system services. Applications do not solicit or possess:

  • Internet connectivity or network state surveillance permissions.
  • Camera, optical imaging, or optical sensor access.
  • Microphone, audio capture, or acoustic monitoring access.
  • Contact directories, address records, or messaging logs.
  • Shared volume read/write rights across external storage mediums.

5. Complete Exclusion of Third-Party Libraries

To ensure zero exposure to tracking vectors or algorithmic profiling, software distributed by the Company contains:

  • Zero advertising display libraries or monetization partner SDKs.
  • Zero behavioral analytics frameworks (e.g., Firebase, Mixpanel, Segment).
  • Zero federated authentication services or social graph connectors.
  • Zero remote database clients or cloud-synchronization handlers.

6. Protection of Minors

Our software tools do not collect information from any individual regardless of age, fully complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and related child privacy standards globally.

7. Amendments to This Policy

In the event that our technical specifications undergo material changes, revisions to this statement will be published directly on this verified domain alongside an amended revision timestamp.
